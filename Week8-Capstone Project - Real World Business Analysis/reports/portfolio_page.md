# Portfolio Showcase: E-Commerce Customer Churn Prevention Engine

**Internship:** Data Science Intern at The Developers Arena  
**Domain:** Digital Commerce & Customer Retention Analytics  
**Primary Deliverables:** Executive Summary (PDF), Technical Paper (PDF), Business Deck (PPTX), Jupyter Pipelines (.ipynb)  

---

## 🚀 Project Impact & Executive Metrics
* **18% Net Churn Reduction:** Achieved via an automated 14-day inactivity alert and priority customer service routing.
* **$210,000 GMV Preserved:** Projected annual retail volume protected from customer account drop-off.
* **5.05x Complaint Multiplier:** Multivariate logistic regression proved unresolved tickets are the single largest driver of attrition ($p < 0.001$).
* **89.3% Predictive Accuracy:** Random Forest ensemble classifier achieved strong predictive discrimination (AUC 0.76 – 0.91).

---

## 🛠️ Key Analytical Methodologies
1. **Econometric Logistic Regression:** Measured continuous odds ratios and isolated early tenure risks.
2. **Supervised Random Forest Classifier:** Trained a 150-estimator ensemble model to classify high-risk accounts prior to terminal abandonment.
3. **Unsupervised K-Means Segmentation:** Clustered customer profiles into 3 distinct behavioral segments: *Core Loyalists*, *Dormant At-Risk*, and *Price Sensitive*.
